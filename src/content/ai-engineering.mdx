## What changed, and what did not

The section was expanded at the redesign because one comma-separated line of
five words read as bare next to the rest of the page. `summary` is a new
optional field carrying prose.

Two constraints from ADR-1 survive that expansion unchanged, and are easy to
break by accident:

- **No links, on any item — including this repository.** Linking was considered
  and explicitly declined at Application Design Q6, because it would cross the
  line `requirements.md` Q8 drew: no cross-reference to this site's own build
  process. `AIEngineeringSection.test.tsx` asserts the rendered section contains
  zero anchors, so the decision is guarded rather than merely documented.
- **No card grid.** The tagged-grid presentation was Option A in ADR-1 and was
  rejected: it implies each item has a linkable destination, which is not true
  yet. `items` therefore stays a single text node with no element children —
  also asserted.

## A note on Vynkor

`requirements.md` Q2 confirmed that Vynkor should stay "as vague as possible",
deliberately so a reader does not conclude it competes with a full-time role.
That constraint was written for the **Experience** section, where the Vynkor
sub-line under Point & Pay is still one short line and still vague.

Naming Vynkor's actual subject matter here is a knowing change to how much is
disclosed, made by the site's owner. The two treatments are now deliberately
different: brief inside the employment history, substantive in the section
about independent engineering work. Anyone revisiting Q2 should read it as
scoped to the Experience sub-line rather than to the whole site.
